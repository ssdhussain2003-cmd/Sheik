About

Who I Am

I am a Results-driven Cyber Security Analyst with 1+ year of hands-on experience at Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) in SOC L1 operations, threat detection, and incident response across multiple enterprise client environments.

My daily work involves monitoring and triaging security alerts using CrowdStrike Falcon Insight, IBM QRadar SIEM, Palo Alto Cortex XSOAR, and Cortex XDR — performing incident triage, IOC investigation, log analysis, and escalation following SOC playbooks.

What sets me apart is a unique combination of security operations experience, published IEEE research, real-world vulnerability discovery through bug bounty programs, and a developer background that gives me a deeper understanding of how applications are built — and where they break.

I am primarily targeting SOC Analyst, Threat Detection, and Incident Response roles, while remaining open to penetration testing and threat intelligence opportunities.
